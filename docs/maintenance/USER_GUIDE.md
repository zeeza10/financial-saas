**Purpose:** Outline the end-user guide. It will be completed as features are implemented; it must never describe features that do not exist.

**Related:** `USER_FLOWS.md`, `USER_ROLES.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `BUSINESS_RULES.md`

Status: outline only. Content will be written when each feature ships, in simple language, with screenshots from the real product.

1. Getting started: create an account, enable MFA, create an organization, choose base currency.
2. Understanding your books: accounts, business vs personal money, why transfers and investor money are not income.
3. Adding accounts and importing transactions; reviewing duplicates.
4. Posting and correcting entries (why nothing is edited or deleted; how reversals work).
5. Reconciling a bank statement.
6. Closing a period.
7. Reports: Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow.
8. Investors, profit sharing and individual ledgers (when released).
9. Team members and roles; security tips (MFA, sessions, API keys).
10. Exporting data; getting help; reporting a security issue.
